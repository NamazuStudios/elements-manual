<h6 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-use-extensions-to-find-advanced-mappings">Use Extensions to find Advanced Mappings</h6>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>No fields may start with a <code>.</code> character in Elements. Therefore all query fields beginning with a <code>.</code> are used to trigger operators to assist in finding documents. Special operators come into play when dealing with single object mapping.</p>
