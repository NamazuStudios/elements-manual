<h1>The Design Notebook</h1>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Design Notebook is Namazu Cloud's design-first home for your game's design. You write your Game Design Documents as structured Markdown, gather the files and art that inform them, track the questions and decisions a design collects as it moves from draft to shipped, and let your AI agents read and contribute to all of it alongside you.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A <strong>notebook</strong> belongs to your organization and holds <strong>pages</strong>: the individual Game Design Documents (GDDs) that make up the design, plus the design files, asset entries, and GitHub tickets that support them. Every member of the organization sees the same notebooks, so a notebook is a shared workspace rather than a personal scratchpad.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-the-notebook-gives-you">What the notebook gives you</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Structured Markdown pages.</strong> A page is written as ordinary Markdown and stored as sections, one per heading. Edits, notes, statuses, and history attach to the part of the design they concern, not to a faceless file blob. See <a href="design-notebook-editor">Writing a page: the GDD editor</a>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Section statuses and locks.</strong> Every section carries a lifecycle status from draft to shipped, with a lock for what is truly settled. The status tells teammates, and your agents, what kind of attention a section needs next. See <a href="design-notebook-statuses">Section statuses and locks</a>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Notes and question notes.</strong> Leave remarks anchored to exact lines of the design, ask open questions that anyone can answer, and answer the questions your agents leave for you. See <a href="design-notebook-notes">Notes and question notes</a>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Revision history.</strong> Every save writes immutable per-section revisions. Recall and compare any section's history without leaving the editor, and follow a section across renames and moves.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Design files and assets.</strong> Upload reference art, audio, and documents as revisioned files, link them into pages where they render inline, and track deliverables whose reference art is pinned to an exact revision. See <a href="design-notebook-files-assets-tickets">Files, assets, and tickets</a>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Agents as collaborators.</strong> A dedicated MCP surface exposes the notebook to your agents, guided by per-item instructions and by stage defaults, so an agent follows the written design instead of improvising. See <a href="design-notebook-agents">Working with agents</a>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>A recoverable delete model.</strong> Deleting archives rather than destroys: pages, files, and notes can be restored until an explicit purge, which is reserved to owners and admins.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="where-to-find-it">Where to find it</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Open the Namazu Cloud portal and choose <strong>Design</strong> in the navigation. The Design page fills the window: the notebook tree on the left lists your organization's notebooks and, under each, its pages, files, assets, and tickets. Selecting a page opens the editor on the right.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A guided tour walks first-time users through the whole loop: the notebook, a first page, writing Markdown with media, reading in document mode, leaving a note, setting a status, locking what is settled, saving, and watching history build.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-can-do-what">Who can do what</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Members</strong> of an organization can read and edit everything in that organization's notebooks: writing and saving pages, leaving notes and questions, setting most section statuses, uploading and replacing files, and tracking assets and tickets.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Owners and admins</strong> additionally approve a design for building (the greenlit status) and perform the destructive acts: archiving notebooks, pages, and files, and purging what has been archived.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
