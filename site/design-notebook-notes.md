<h1>Notes and Question Notes</h1>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Notes are how a design collects its conversation: remarks from artists, writers, producers, and agents, attached to the section they concern and, when it matters, to the exact lines. Questions are a special kind of note: an open question about the design that anyone can answer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="remarks">Remarks</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Select a section and open the panel's <strong>Notes</strong> tab. Type into the box and press Enter or <strong>Add</strong> to leave a remark. Notes list newest first with their author and the revision they were left on; click a note to expand its full text.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="anchored-notes">Anchored notes</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Right-click in the editor and choose <strong>Add note on line…</strong> (or right-click a section and choose <strong>Add note</strong>) to anchor a note to a line range, the selection you made when you wrote it. Anchored notes show a line chip in the list; click it to jump to those lines in the buffer, highlighted for a few seconds, without leaving the section you are reading.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="pending-until-save">Pending until save</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A note you write is held as pending, marked with a pending dot, and flushes with the next save. This is deliberate: notes land in the same transaction as the Markdown they refer to, and a note's revision is the revision of the design its author was looking at. Pending notes are never lost: they ride the page's draft like unsaved edits, and they are visible immediately in the panel, with an undo affordance until the save commits them. After the save, the panel shows them as ordinary persisted notes without needing a refresh.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="questions-and-replies">Questions and replies</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Toggle the <strong>?</strong> beside the note input (or the same toggle in the right-click note dialog) to ask an open question instead of leaving a remark. Questions are the notebook's designated place for the things a design has not decided yet: they render amber with a question badge, stand out from ordinary remarks, and can be answered in place.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Anyone may answer: you, a teammate, or your agent. Open a question and reply in the box beneath it; replies nest under their question on the question's own section. Agents are explicitly instructed to ask their open questions as question notes rather than summarizing or burying them in prose, so the questions an agent leaves are the ones you see and answer here.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Replying does not by itself resolve a question: a reply may itself be a clarifying question. Resolve explicitly when the answer settles it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="the-questions-view">The Questions view</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The editor header's <strong>? Questions</strong> opens one list of every open question across the whole page: who asked it, where it lives (the section it is attached to, one click to jump there), its replies so far, and a reply box and resolve control in place. Nothing hides section by section; when the list is empty, the page has no open questions. A toggle shows resolved questions as well, struck through.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="resolving-deleting-and-recovery">Resolving, deleting, and recovery</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Resolve</strong> strikes a question or note through while keeping it visible; <strong>reopen</strong> restores it. <strong>Delete</strong> on a resolved note archives it: archived notes sit behind the panel's <strong>Show archived notes</strong> link with a restore control, and nothing is silently destroyed. An unanswered question stays in the Questions view until it is resolved or deleted.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
