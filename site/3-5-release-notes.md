<h1>3.5 Release Notes</h1>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":1} -->
<h1 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-namazu-elements-3-5-release-notes">Namazu Elements 3.5 Release Notes</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-faster-insights-stronger-security-and-smoother-operations">Faster insights, stronger security, and smoother operations</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Namazu Elements 3.5 is here, bringing powerful new capabilities that<br>make your game backend easier to manage, more secure, and more<br>transparent. This release delivers major quality of life improvements<br>for developers and operators, along with new tools that help teams move<br>faster and build with confidence.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-highlights">Highlights</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-unlock-advanced-workflows-with-direct-database-access">Unlock advanced workflows with Direct Database Access</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can now access MongoDB directly from Custom Code, giving you a new<br>level of flexibility for complex logic, data analysis, and custom<br>integrations. This feature opens the door to deeper control over your<br>data and faster iteration on gameplay systems. Learn more:<br><a href="https://namazustudios.com/docs/custom-code/direct-mongodb-access-3-5/">https://namazustudios.com/docs/custom-code/direct-mongodb-access-3-5/</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-cleaner-and-clearer-player-management">Cleaner and clearer player management</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Disabled users no longer appear in the user list once removed. This<br>update cuts down on confusion and keeps your management dashboards<br>focused on active players.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-find-what-you-need-instantly">Find what you need, instantly</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Improved searches makes it easy to locate items across even the<br>largest collections. Teams can now diagnose issues, verify<br>configurations, and assist players much more quickly.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-better-visibility-across-your-installation">Better visibility across your installation</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Version 3.5 introduces improved reporting on installed Elements so you<br>can get a clear picture of what is running in your environment at any<br>time. This supports faster audits and more reliable deployments.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-stronger-protection-for-sensitive-data">Stronger protection for sensitive data</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sensitive information is now automatically redacted in logs and system<br>properties. Your data is safer, compliance is easier, and logs are<br>cleaner for debugging.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-more-helpful-startup-insights">More helpful startup insights</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Startup logs now include key configuration details, giving developers<br>and operators a clearer understanding of how the system initializes and<br>how their environment is configured.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-fixes-and-polish">Fixes and polish</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This release includes a set of reliability improvements across the<br>new UI. Highlights include:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Smoother UI workflows, including fixes for metadata editing, large<br>object deletion, and other usability issues.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Reliable creation, modification, and deletion of leaderboards.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Resolution of a 500 error that could occur during logout or session<br>destruction.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
