<h1>Custom Elements</h1>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Custom Elements allow you to extend Elements by running your own authoritative code inside the platform.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>They are best thought of as <strong>server-side plugins</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-a-custom-element-is">What a Custom Element Is</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A Custom Element:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>is a server-side application</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>runs in a trusted environment</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>can access Elements data and systems</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>cannot be modified by players</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-when-you-might-use-a-custom-element">When You Might Use a Custom Element</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Custom Elements are a good fit when:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>you want to avoid running your own backend</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>logic is tightly coupled to Elements features</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>functionality should be reusable across games</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>third parties need to integrate cleanly</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Examples include:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>receipt validation</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>promotional reward systems</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>scheduled content rotation</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>anti‑fraud or validation logic</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-how-custom-elements-fit-into-the-system">How Custom Elements Fit Into the System</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Custom Elements participate in the same authoritative flows as external servers:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":22405,"width":"382px","height":"auto","aspectRatio":"1.8571872732925692","sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none"} -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From the client’s perspective, nothing changes — authority remains on the server.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-this-means-for-developers">What This Means for Developers</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you’re new to backend development:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Custom Elements can significantly reduce the amount of infrastructure you need</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>You still write server‑style code, but Elements handles the environment</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you already run a backend:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Custom Elements allow you to move shared or reusable logic closer to the data</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Both approaches are fully supported.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
